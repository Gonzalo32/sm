Session calculations are derived from authoritative CME Group documentation (CME Rulebook Chapter 5 & Globex Trading Schedule):

```text
SESSION_STRUCTURE:         Sunday 18:00 ET to Friday 17:00 ET
DAILY_MAINTENANCE_BREAK:  Monday–Thursday 17:00 ET to 18:00 ET (60 minutes)
TRADABLE_DAILY_HOURS:     23 Hours / Day (1,380 Tradable Minutes / Day)
RTH_WINDOW:               09:30 ET to 16:00 ET (390 Minutes / Day)
ETH_WINDOW:               18:00 ET (prev day) to 09:30 ET & 16:00 ET to 17:00 ET (990 Minutes / Day)
DST_AWARENESS:            Fixed local ET schedule. UTC offsets transition between UTC-4 (EDT) and UTC-5 (EST).
```

### UTC Transition Example across DST:
- **EDT (Summer):** Daily Maintenance Break occurs from `21:00 UTC` to `22:00 UTC`.
- **EST (Winter):** Daily Maintenance Break occurs from `22:00 UTC` to `23:00 UTC`.

## 4. RECONCILED SESSION & VOLUME MODELS (A, B, & C)

CP33.7.2 maintains three strict, non-interchangeable volume models:

### Model A — Theoretical Calendar Grid (`THEORETICAL_CALENDAR_GRID`)
$$\text{Records} = \text{Days} \times 24 \times 60 = \text{Days} \times 1,440$$

### Model B — CME Calendar-Aware Session Grid (`CME_CALENDAR_AWARE_SESSION_GRID`)
$$\text{Session Minutes} = (\text{Trading Days} - \text{Holidays}) \times 1,380$$

### Model C — Observed Sample Density (`OBSERVED_SAMPLE_DENSITY`)
- **Databento `ohlcv-1m` Trade Bar Rule:** Databento emits `ohlcv-1m` aggregate bars only for minutes during which at least one trade occurred. Omitted zero-volume intervals do **NOT** indicate data loss or corruption:
$$\text{OBSERVED_RECORDS} \le \text{THEORETICAL_SESSION_MINUTES}$$
