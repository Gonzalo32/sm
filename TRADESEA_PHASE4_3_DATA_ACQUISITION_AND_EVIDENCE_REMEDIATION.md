## 4. Preregistered v2.0 Predictive Validation Protocol Design

To eliminate lookahead bias, data snooping, and parameter overfitting, the following validation protocol is preregistered. All parameters, thresholds, and selection criteria are frozen **before** acquiring or inspecting external data.

### 4.1 Instrument Hierarchy & Anti-Collinearity Controls
1. **Primary Evaluation Instrument (MNQ):**
   - Formal statistical hypothesis testing ($N \ge 100$, Target Hit Rate $\ge 52\%$, Realized Net R:R $\ge 1.25$) will be conducted **strictly and exclusively on Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (`MNQ`)**.
   - MNQ reflects real retail contract specifications ($2.00 per full point, $0.50 per tick).
2. **Secondary Cross-Contract Cohort (NQ):**
   - Full E-mini Nasdaq-100 (`NQ`) will be processed as a parallel replication cohort to verify that signals are not distorted by micro-contract liquidity differences.
   - **Strict Anti-Pooling Rule:** NQ and MNQ trades evaluated on the same calendar period **will never be pooled together** to artificially inflate sample size ($N$).

### 4.2 Calendar Sampling & Independence Architecture
1. **Temporal Horizon:** At least **10 distinct CME Globex trading sessions** spanning at least **2 distinct calendar months** (e.g., 5 sessions from December 2025, 5 sessions from February 2026).
2. **Session Boundaries:** Evaluation is strictly restricted to CME Regular Trading Hours (RTH, 13:30 to 20:00 UTC / 09:30 to 16:00 EDT). Overnight and pre-market sessions (ETH) are excluded to prevent thin-liquidity distortions.
3. **Initial Sample Gate:** The evaluation must yield a minimum of **$N = 100$ eligible non-overlapping scenarios**.
   - *Statistical Precaution:* Achieving $N=100$ satisfies the minimum sample gate to run statistical tests; it does **not** assert that $N=100$ guarantees high statistical power for rare breakout events.
4. **Contract Rollover Protocol:** Only active front-month contracts with peak open interest and volume are evaluated. Days immediately adjacent to expiration (quarterly roll week) are excluded to avoid roll-spread distortions.

### 4.3 Why Non-Overlapping Windows Do Not Guarantee Statistical Independence
A critical theoretical requirement of Phase 4.3 is documenting why non-overlapping forward windows ($T_{\text{res}} \le T_{\text{next}}$) eliminate horizon overlap artifacts but **do not guarantee statistical independence ($i.i.d.$)**:

1. **Persistent Market Regimes (Autoregressive Volatility / ARCH):**
   - Financial markets exhibit volatility clustering and regime persistence. If Trade $A$ and Trade $B$ occur on the same day during a high-volatility trend regime, their return variances $\sigma_A^2$ and $\sigma_B^2$ are drawn from the same underlying latent volatility state.
2. **Macroeconomic Common Shocks:**
   - A single macroeconomic event (e.g., FOMC rate release at 18:00 UTC, CPI release, or geopolitical event) permanently alters the price trajectory for the remainder of the trading day. Multiple non-overlapping trades taken after the release are subject to the same common macro shock.
3. **Liquidity Depletion & State Memory:**
   - When a major liquidity pool (SSL/BSL) is swept on Trade $A$, resting market depth is depleted. Trade $B$, registered 30 minutes later, operates in an altered liquidity landscape directly created by Trade $A$.
4. **Methodological Mitigation:**
   - To account for residual intra-day dependence, hypothesis testing will incorporate **Session-Level Block Bootstrapping** (resampling entire daily blocks of trades rather than individual trades) and calculate the **Effective Sample Size ($\text{ESS}$)**:
     $$\text{ESS} = \frac{N}{1 + 2 \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \hat{\rho}_k}$$

### 4.4 Causal Forecast Registration & Resolution Rules
1. **Point-in-Time Setup Freezing:**
   - At bar $T_0$, lookback candles $[T_{0-120} \dots T_0]$ are analyzed by `ICTEngine`, `ConfluenceEngine`, and `MarketAnalystEngine`.
   - If an analysis is produced with status `ANALYSIS_READY`, the forecast is registered immutably:
     $$\mathcal{F}(T_0) = \left\{ \text{Direction, RefPrice } Close(T_0), \text{NearbyTarget, ExtendedTarget, Invalidation, EstRR} \right\}$$
2. **Forward Evaluation Horizon:**
   - Horizon is capped at **60 bars** (60 minutes).
   - Intrabar evaluation checks High and Low of subsequent bars ($T_1 \dots T_{60}$).
3. **Ambiguous Hit Rule (Fail-Closed):**
   - If both Nearby Target and Invalidation are touched on the exact same 1m candle, the outcome is classified as `AMBIGUOUS_EVALUATION` and penalized as a full invalidation loss.
4. **Unresolved & Boundary Rule:**
   - If a forecast reaches bar 60 without hitting target or invalidation, it is marked `EXPIRED` and evaluated at mark-to-market close.
   - If a forecast is active when the RTH session closes (20:00 UTC), it is marked `UNRESOLVED` and evaluated at the final RTH close. Unresolved trades are separated from categorical target-hit calculations.
5. **Friction Tiers:**
   - Results will be reported across three friction assumptions:
     - **Gross Move:** $0.00$ pt deduction.
     - **Baseline Friction:** $1.00$ pt deduction ($4 \times 0.25$ pt ticks per round turn).
     - **Stress Friction:** $1.75$ pt deduction ($7 \times 0.25$ pt ticks per round turn).

### 4.5 Anti-Tuning & Governance Freeze Rule
* **Zero Post-Hoc Parameter Optimization:** No decision thresholds, weights, or model parameters in `core/ict/` or `core/analyst/` may be altered based on observed validation outcomes.
* **Preregistered Decision Criteria:**
  - **Criterion 1 (Directional Hit Rate):** Target Hit Rate $\ge 52.0\%$ under baseline friction.
  - **Criterion 2 (Realized Net Payoff):** Realized Net Reward/Risk $\ge 1.25$.
  - **Criterion 3 (Ambiguity Rate):** Ambiguous touches $\le 8.0\%$.
  - **Criterion 4 (Residual Autocorrelation):** $|\hat{\rho}_1| \le 0.15$ with block bootstrap significance.
  - If any criterion fails on authentic data, the failure must be reported directly without tuning model logic to force a pass.
