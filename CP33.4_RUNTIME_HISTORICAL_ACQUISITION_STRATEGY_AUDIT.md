## 1. Executive Objective & Central Question

Checkpoint **CP33.4** performed a forensic, implementation-independent audit of the TradeSea runtime environment to determine how deep historical market data can be acquired, accumulated, and reproduced without external data providers.

### Central Architectural Question Answered:
> **What is technically possible today using the real TradeSea runtime/chart/WebSocket environment, and what architecture is required to progressively build a reproducible historical dataset without external market-data providers?**

$$\mathbf{FACTUAL\ ANSWER:}$$

1. **TradeSea Direct Initial Depth**: Bounded strictly to **31 candles** per timeframe series push (`timescale_update`).
2. **Chart Scrollback Behavior**: Scrolling left shifts the canvas viewport offset over the loaded 31 candles. It emits **zero** WebSocket frame requests or older candle payloads.
3. **Progressive Accumulation Capability**: The extension can build a deep historical dataset over elapsed time by accumulating live closed candles at a rate of **288 bars/day (5m timeframe)** into `CandleStore.ts` and persisting them to local storage.
4. **Timeframe Rotation**: Changing timeframe (e.g. 5m $\rightarrow$ 15m $\rightarrow$ 1H $\rightarrow$ 1D) delivers a fresh 31-candle window for that timeframe series.
