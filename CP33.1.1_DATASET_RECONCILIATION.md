## 1. Executive Summary

CP33.1.1 audited the exact dataset population used during the real market replay validation in CP33.1. This report resolves the exact breakdown of scenario counts, candle totals, timeframes, and timestamp boundaries.

Key Findings:
1. **Scenario & Candle Population:** The dataset is generated from `CP21DatasetGenerator` using frozen historical market data feeds (`MNQ` and `NQ`). It comprises exactly 500 scenarios, each containing 50 future candles, yielding a total population of **25,000 real market candles**.
2. **Instrument Distribution:** Exactly 250 scenario series for `MNQ` (12,500 candles) and 250 scenario series for `NQ` (12,500 candles).
3. **Timeframe Breakdown:** 200 series on `1m` (10,000 candles), 200 series on `5m` (10,000 candles), and 100 series on `15m` (5,000 candles).
4. **Data Overlap Disclosure:** Each scenario series is an isolated temporal slice generated with non-overlapping index windows across `EXPLORATION` (100 series), `SELECTION` (200 series), and `HOLDOUT` (200 series) regimes.
