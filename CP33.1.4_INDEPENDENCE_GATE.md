## 1. Synthetic Contamination Gate Audit

To prevent false real-market claims, the contamination gate enforces strict separation between benchmark synthetic datasets and actual real-market datasets:

```text
SYNTHETIC_DATASET_USED_FOR_CP33 = YES
SYNTHETIC_DATASET_ACCEPTABLE_FOR_REAL_VALIDATION = NO
SYNTHETIC_CONTAMINATION_GATE = PASS
```

- **Explanation:** The test suite verifies that `CP21DatasetGenerator.ts` is explicitly recognized as a synthetic generator. Any attempt to pass synthetic trigonometric data as real market data is blocked by the gate.

---

## 2. Dataset Independence Requirements

Future candidate real-market datasets must be completely independent from:
1. `CP21DatasetGenerator.ts`
2. F10 Human Review Pack (`CP32_REVIEW_PACK/cases.json`)
3. F11 Stratified Sample Pack (`CP32.6_HUMAN_REVIEW_PACK/cases.json`)
4. Checkpoints CP33.1, CP33.1.1, CP33.1.2, and CP33.1.3 scenario items

If a dataset derives from any of the above synthetic sources:
```text
REAL_DATASET_INDEPENDENCE = NO
```

---

## 3. Cross-Instrument & Cross-Timeframe Requirements

### A. Cross-Instrument Independence (MNQ vs NQ)
- MNQ and NQ datasets must originate from independent raw exchange feeds (CME Globex).
- Timestamps, volume, and tick movements must reflect true independent market transactions, rather than scalar shifts of the same mathematical curve.

### B. Cross-Timeframe Derivation Hierarchy
- **1m Data:** Native exchange 1-minute bar feeds (`REAL_NATIVE_DATA`).
- **5m / 15m Data:** Either native multi-timeframe feeds (`REAL_NATIVE_DATA`) or deterministically aggregated bar resampling derived from 1m real native data (`REAL_MARKET_DERIVED`).
