## 2. Key Differentiation

```text
REAL_SOURCE_IMPLEMENTED = YES
REAL_SOURCE_EXECUTED = NO
REAL_DATA_RECEIVED = NO
```

- **Implemented vs Executed vs Received:**
  - `REAL_SOURCE_IMPLEMENTED = YES`: The architecture (`MarketDataAdapter.ts`, `CandleStore.ts`, `pageBridge.ts`, `ICTPipelineCoordinator.ts`) is fully implemented to receive and process real market feeds.
  - `REAL_SOURCE_EXECUTED = NO`: No live connection was executed during test suite run.
  - `REAL_DATA_RECEIVED = NO`: Zero real market candles were received during headless build/test execution.
