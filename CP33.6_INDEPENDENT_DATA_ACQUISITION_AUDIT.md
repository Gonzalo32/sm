## 8. DERIVED TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION AUDIT (OPTION B)

Option B mathematical aggregation was executed from base `1m` candles to construct `5m`, `15m`, and `1H` series according to strict rules:

$$\text{Open} = \text{Open}(\text{bar}_1)$$
$$\text{High} = \max_{i=1..N} (\text{High}_i)$$
$$\text{Low} = \min_{i=1..N} (\text{Low}_i)$$
$$\text{Close} = \text{Close}(\text{bar}_N)$$
$$\text{Volume} = \sum_{i=1..N} (\text{Volume}_i)$$

### Deterministic Aggregation Verification:
- **Execution Test:** Aggregation pipeline executed twice independently on the normalized `1m` dataset.
- **Result:** Identical SHA-256 output produced on both runs (`DETERMINISTIC_AGGREGATION = PASS`).
