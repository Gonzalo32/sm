## 4. MATHEMATICAL VOLUME RECONCILIATION MODELS

CP33.7.1 reconciles expected 1-minute record counts across three distinct mathematical models:

### Model A — 24-Hour Continuous Minute Grid (Theoretical Calendar Grid)
$$\text{Records} = \text{Days} \times 24 \times 60 = \text{Days} \times 1,440$$

### Model B — CME Globex Session-Adjusted Grid (Exchange Schedule Grid)
$$\text{Records} = \text{Trading Days} \times 23 \times 60 = \text{Trading Days} \times 1,380$$
*(Accounts for 5 trading days/week and excludes the 1-hour daily maintenance pause from 17:00 to 18:00 ET).*

### Model C — Observed Data Density (Empirical Trade Bar Model)
- **Databento `GLBX.MDP3` `ohlcv-1m` Rule:** Databento emits `ohlcv-1m` aggregate bars only for minutes during which at least one trade occurred. Empty intervals with zero trade volume are omitted from the data feed:
$$\text{RECORD_COUNT} \le \text{MINUTE_GRID_COUNT}$$
