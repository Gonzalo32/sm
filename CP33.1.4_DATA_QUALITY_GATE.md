**Production Mutation:** NONE (Read-Only Audit)

---

## 1. Mandatory Data Quality Validation Rules

Any future acquired real-market dataset must pass 100% of the following automated quality checks prior to being ingested into the backtesting engine:

### Check 1: Geometric OHLC Sanity
- $\text{high} \ge \max(\text{open}, \text{close})$
- $\text{low} \le \min(\text{open}, \text{close})$
- $\text{high} \ge \text{low}$
- $\text{open} > 0, \text{high} > 0, \text{low} > 0, \text{close} > 0$

### Check 2: Timestamp Monotonicity & Uniqueness
- Strictly increasing timestamps: $t_k > t_{k-1}$ for all sequential candles $k$.
- Duplicate timestamps for the same instrument and timeframe are strictly forbidden.

### Check 3: Interval & Timeframe Coherence
- Time intervals between candles must match the declared timeframe (e.g. 60,000 ms for 1m, 300,000 ms for 5m, 900,000 ms for 15m), accounting only for standard market session closures.

### Check 4: Data Completeness & Type Safety
- Zero `NaN`, `null`, `undefined`, or string-coerced numeric values.
- Zero missing candles during active market session hours without logged feed gaps.

### Check 5: Synthetic Signature Rejection
- Datasets containing trigonometric patterns (`Math.sin`, `Math.cos`), repeating index seeds, or mock fixtures must be rejected automatically.

---

## 2. Policy for Corrupt Data

```text
AUTOMATED_CORRECTION = NO
```

- **Strict Rejection Policy:** If any candle in a dataset fails any quality check, the backtest suite must **NOT** automatically mutate, interpolate, smoothing-adjust, or repair the data.
- **Outcome:** The dataset must be immediately assigned `DATASET_STATUS = REJECTED`.
