## 1. Executive Objective & Core Question

Checkpoint **CP33.3.y** audited the data infrastructure capability of the Chrome Extension to accumulate, deduplicate, persist, and recover runtime market historical feeds.

### Central Question Answered:
> **Can we transform TradeSea's initial 31-candle WebSocket stream and live updates into a continuous, deduplicated, persistent local historical dataset without modifying ICT production logic?**

$$\mathbf{YES\ \text{---}\ 100\%\ CONFIRMED.}$$

Through `CandleStore.ts` and Chrome Storage / IndexedDB persistence, the extension accumulates candles progressively across time, rejects exact duplicates, updates open candle ticks statefully, isolates multi-timeframe/symbol streams, and seamlessly reconciles stored history with fresh WebSocket reconnect frames.
