## 4. Audit Event Causality

The pipeline causality chain was verified line-by-line in `core/ict/engine/ICTEngine.ts`:

$$\text{RAW\_CANDLE} \longrightarrow \text{DETECTOR} \longrightarrow \text{EVENT} \longrightarrow \text{DOWNSTREAM\_CONSUMER} \longrightarrow \text{HUD/DRAWING}$$

1. **Raw Candle Stream**: Passed to `ICTEngine.process(candles)`.
2. **Detector Step**: `SwingDetector.detectSwings()` identifies pivot highs/lows from candle OHLC boundaries.
3. **Event Generation**: `StructureEngine` and `LiquidityEngine` consume confirmed swings to emit `BOS`, `MSS`, `BSL`, `SSL`, and sweep events.
4. **Downstream Engine Consumption**: `FVGEngine`, `OrderBlockEngine`, and `DisplacementEngine` evaluate candle windows and pass detections to `SetupEngine`.
5. **HUD & Drawing Primitives**: `ICTPipelineCoordinator` transforms market state into `ICT_HUD_STATE_UPDATE` payload and `ICT_DRAWING_OBJECTS` canvas rendering boxes/lines.
