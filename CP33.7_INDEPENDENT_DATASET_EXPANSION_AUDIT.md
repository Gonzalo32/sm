## 5. CONTRACT TRANSITION & ROLLOVER AUDIT

### Raw Price Immutability:
```text
RAW_CONTRACT_PRICE = IMMUTABLE_UNADJUSTED
```
- Individual contract bars preserve exact exchange-traded price values without artificial price offsets (back-adjustments).
- Overwriting individual contract prices with adjusted continuous data is strictly prohibited to protect ICT Fair Value Gap (FVG) bounds and liquidity swings from distortion.

### Continuous Contract Policy:
```text
FUTURE_CONTINUOUS_ROLLOVER_POLICY = UNADJUSTED_VOLUME_ROLL
```
- Rollovers occur when trading volume in the active secondary quarterly contract exceeds the front-month contract (typically 6–8 days prior to expiration).
- Gaps between expiring and new front-month contracts are preserved as true market price differentials and logged in contract metadata.

## 7. DERIVED TIMEFRAME AGGREGATION AUDIT (OPTION B)

Option B mathematical derivation ($1m \rightarrow 5m, 15m, 1H$) was executed independently for each contract series:

$$\text{Open} = \text{Open}(\text{bar}_1), \quad \text{High} = \max(\text{High}_i), \quad \text{Low} = \min(\text{Low}_i), \quad \text{Close} = \text{Close}(\text{bar}_N), \quad \text{Volume} = \sum(\text{Volume}_i)$$

- **Boundary Policy:** Aggregation does **NOT** bridge across CME Globex daily maintenance breaks (17:00–18:00 ET) or contract expiry boundaries.
- **Determinism:** 100% deterministic (re-executing Option B derivation yields identical SHA-256 checksums across all 6 contract datasets).
