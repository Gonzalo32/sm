# S5 — Formal Strategy Specification

## 1. Overview

This document specifies the formal, deterministic strategy tuples for converting frozen ICT candidate signals (`LONG_CANDIDATE`, `SHORT_CANDIDATE`) generated by Models A, B, and C into testable execution hypotheses.

---

## 2. Formal Strategy Tuple Definition

Each strategy hypothesis is defined as an 11-tuple:

```text
STRATEGY_TUPLE = (
    SIGNAL_SOURCE,
    ENTRY_RULE,
    EXIT_RULE,
    RISK_REFERENCE,
    POSITION_STATE_RULE,
    SIGNAL_CONFLICT_RULE,
    COST_INPUTS,
    TIMEFRAME,
    SYMBOL,
    MODEL,
    DIRECTION
)
```

---

## 3. Predefined Strategy Variants

### Variant 1: Baseline Confirmation Close with Horizon Exit (E1-X1)
* **SIGNAL_SOURCE**: Frozen ICT Engine (`core/ict/`)
* **ENTRY_RULE**: `E1_CONFIRMATION_CLOSE` (`entryPrice = confirmation candle close`)
* **EXIT_RULE**: `X1_FIXED_HORIZON` ($H \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20\}$)
* **RISK_REFERENCE**: `SWEEP_EXTREME_OR_FVG_BOUNDARY` from `CandidateContext`
* **POSITION_STATE_RULE**: `SINGLE_POSITION_PER_SYMBOL_TIMEFRAME`
* **SIGNAL_CONFLICT_RULE**: `DETERMINISTIC_FIRST_CONFIRMED_PRIORITY`
* **COST_INPUTS**: Standard Futures (NQ/MNQ contract spec)
* **TIMEFRAME**: 1m, 5m, 15m
* **SYMBOL**: NQ, MNQ
* **MODEL**: `MODEL_A`, `MODEL_B`, `MODEL_C` (Evaluated independently)
* **DIRECTION**: `LONG`, `SHORT` (Evaluated independently)

### Variant 2: Next Candle Open with Symmetric Risk/Reward (E2-X2)
* **SIGNAL_SOURCE**: Frozen ICT Engine (`core/ict/`)
* **ENTRY_RULE**: `E2_NEXT_CANDLE_OPEN` (`entryPrice = first candle open after confirmation`)
* **EXIT_RULE**: `X2_STRUCTURAL_RR` ($1:1$ RR based on structural risk reference)
* **RISK_REFERENCE**: `SWEEP_EXTREME_OR_FVG_BOUNDARY` from `CandidateContext`
* **POSITION_STATE_RULE**: `SINGLE_POSITION_PER_SYMBOL_TIMEFRAME`
* **SIGNAL_CONFLICT_RULE**: `DETERMINISTIC_FIRST_CONFIRMED_PRIORITY`
* **COST_INPUTS**: Standard Futures (NQ/MNQ contract spec)
* **TIMEFRAME**: 1m, 5m, 15m
* **SYMBOL**: NQ, MNQ
* **MODEL**: `MODEL_A`, `MODEL_B`, `MODEL_C` (Evaluated independently)
* **DIRECTION**: `LONG`, `SHORT` (Evaluated independently)

### Variant 3: FVG Retracement with Opposing Signal Reversal (E3-X3)
* **SIGNAL_SOURCE**: Frozen ICT Engine (`core/ict/`)
* **ENTRY_RULE**: `E3_FVG_RETRACEMENT` (`entryPrice = FVG boundary price from CandidateContext`)
* **EXIT_RULE**: `X3_OPPOSING_SIGNAL` (Reversal on opposite confirmed candidate signal)
* **RISK_REFERENCE**: `SWEEP_EXTREME_OR_FVG_BOUNDARY` from `CandidateContext`
* **POSITION_STATE_RULE**: `SINGLE_POSITION_PER_SYMBOL_TIMEFRAME`
* **SIGNAL_CONFLICT_RULE**: `DETERMINISTIC_FIRST_CONFIRMED_PRIORITY`
* **COST_INPUTS**: Standard Futures (NQ/MNQ contract spec)

* **SYMBOL**: NQ, MNQ
* **MODEL**: `MODEL_A`, `MODEL_B`, `MODEL_C` (Evaluated independently)
* **DIRECTION**: `LONG`, `SHORT` (Evaluated independently)
