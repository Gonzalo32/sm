# CP46 Visual Layer Partial-State Audit

## 1. Overview
Audit of `VisualAdapter` behavior when generating visual representation objects from partial, provisional, or updating logical contexts.

---

## 2. Key Audit Properties
1. **Derivation Non-Authoritativeness:** Visual objects generated by `VisualAdapter.adaptStateToVisuals` are strictly derived projections. They do not mutate or back-propagate to candidate domain context.
2. **Provisional Rendering Tagging:** Visual objects derived from provisional contexts carry non-authoritative metadata indicators (`isAuthoritative = false`).
3. **Clean Cleanup:** Obsolete or replaced contexts do not leave orphaned visual objects in visual rendering buffers.
