## 2. No Data Fabrication Policy

```text
NO_DATA_FABRICATION = YES
```

- **Zero Synthetic Padding:** If an active feed returns less than the requested 60-day historical window (e.g. 0 days or 12 days), the system records `actualAvailableLookbackDays = 12.0` without fabricating candles or filling gaps with artificial data.
- **Synthetic Runtime Blocker (`SYNTHETIC_RUNTIME_BLOCKED = YES`)**: Any attempt to pass `CP21DatasetGenerator` synthetic data into real runtime mode results in an immediate `REJECTED_FOR_REAL_RUNTIME` status.
