# CP33.1.4 — Real Market Data Acquisition & Provenance Requirements Specification

**Audit Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Scope:** Specification of Technical, Geometric & Provenance Requirements for Future Real Market Datasets  
**Production Mutation:** NONE (Read-Only Audit)

---

## 1. Required Real Dataset Specification

To conduct a genuinely valid real-market backtest, any future acquired dataset must fulfill the following mandatory specification:

### A. Target Instruments
- **Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ)**
- **E-mini Nasdaq-100 (NQ)**

### B. Target Timeframes
- **1-minute (1m)**
- **5-minute (5m)**
- **15-minute (15m)**

### C. Required Candle Schema
Every record must contain the following typed fields:
- `timestamp`: Integer (Epoch milliseconds, UTC)
- `open`: Floating-point number (Price)
- `high`: Floating-point number (Price)
- `low`: Floating-point number (Price)
- `close`: Floating-point number (Price)
- `volume`: Integer (Contract volume, when available)

### D. Required Metadata Header
Every imported historical file or stream snapshot must include a immutable metadata manifest:
- `instrument`: Instrument ticker symbol (`MNQ` or `NQ`)
- `timeframe`: Timeframe label (`1m`, `5m`, `15m`)
- `source`: Explicit data provider identifier (e.g. `CME_HISTORICAL`, `RITHMIC_HISTORICAL_API`, `TRADESEA_HISTORICAL_REST`)
- `timezone`: Session timezone (e.g. `UTC`, `EST`)
- `sha256`: SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the raw immutable file
- `acquisitionDate`: ISO timestamp of acquisition

---

## 2. Statistical & Volume Requirements

- **Minimum Candle Count:** At least 1,000 continuous sequential candles per instrument/timeframe pair to ensure statistical confidence.
- **Session Coverage:** Must represent real continuous market trading sessions (e.g. Regular Trading Hours or 23-hour Electronic Trading Hours).

---

## 3. Immutability & Traceability Rules

1. **Read-Only Ingestion:** The raw dataset file must remain untransformed and read-only on disk.
2. **SHA-256 Verification:** Prior to any evaluation, the SHA-256 hash must be verified against the registered acquisition manifest.
3. **No Synthetic Augmentation:** Real datasets must NOT be mixed or filled with synthetic mathematical sine curves or mock candles.
