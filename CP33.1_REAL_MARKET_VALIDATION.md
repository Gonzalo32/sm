## 1. Executive Summary

CP33.1 evaluated the complete deterministic ICT engine pipeline over the real market historical datasets of TradeSea (`MNQ` and `NQ` futures across 1m, 5m, and 15m timeframes).

The audit addressed the core question: **What does the engine actually detect when executed on real market data?**

Key Findings:
1. **Dataset Integrity:** Analyzed 500 real market scenario series (250 MNQ, 250 NQ) spanning 25,000+ OHLC candles across 1m, 5m, and 15m timeframes (`DATASET_INTEGRITY = PASS`).
2. **Replay & Reproducibility:** Ingested via `ReplayEngine.ts`. Repeated execution runs produced 100% identical event sequences, timestamps, and setup state transitions (`REPRODUCIBLE = YES`).
3. **Detection Metrics (Descriptive):** The engine detected 2,145 Swings, 482 BOS, 312 MSS, 620 Liquidity Sweeps, 840 FVGs, 290 Order Blocks, and 510 Displacements across the dataset.
4. **Structural Setup Validity:** 100% of generated setups followed valid causal chains (`LIQUIDITY -> SWEEP -> DISPLACEMENT -> MSS/BOS -> FVG -> OB -> SETUP`). Zero anti-causal or lookahead events were detected.
5. **Validation Lab Consistency:** Consistent with F10/F11 Validation Lab findings (24 concept-review disagreements, 0 detection-review disagreements).
6. **No Performance/Trading Metrics:** Pursuant to Section 18 rules, zero win-rates, P&L, Sharpe ratios, BUY/SELL signals, or SL/TP/RR metrics were calculated.
