## 1. Executive Summary & Core Finding

CP33.1.2 resolved the quantitative denominator difference between the **25,000 candles** reported in CP33.1 and the **10,000 candles** reported in CP33.1.1.

### Technical Resolution:
- **25,000 Candles (`INPUT_CANDLES`):** The total input OHLC candle population across all 500 scenario series windows ($500 \text{ scenarios} \times 50 \text{ input candles per scenario window} = 25,000 \text{ candles}$). This includes pre-event context, warm-up bars, and scenario structure.
- **10,000 Candles (`REPLAY_FUTURE_CANDLES`):** The future replay evaluation window contained in `item.futureCandles` across the 500 scenarios ($500 \text{ scenarios} \times 20 \text{ future candles per scenario} = 10,000 \text{ candles}$).

Both numbers are **100% mathematically correct and reproducible** when their respective scope is properly specified:
$$\text{Input OHLC Population} = 25,000 \quad \vert \quad \text{Replay Future Evaluation Window} = 10,000$$
