## Executive Summary

Audit **CP33.1.12** conducted a forensic evidence audit to resolve a methodological inconsistency identified during CP33.1.11.

Specifically, CP33.1.11 reported `create_series` as the client-side trigger preceding the 31-candle `timescale_update` payload, while simultaneously declaring `WEBSOCKET_SEND_OBSERVED = NO`.

This audit investigated two central questions:
1. **How was the CLIENT$\rightarrow$SERVER message `create_series` actually observed?**
2. **Is there direct, reproducible runtime network capture demonstrating that `create_series` is emitted by TradeSea prior to `timescale_update`?**

---

## 1. Methodological Discrepancy Resolution

* **Current Runtime Bridge Capability (`extension/content/pageBridge.ts`)**:
  `pageBridge.ts` monkey-patches `window.WebSocket` constructor and attaches a passive `addEventListener('message', ...)` listener. It does **NOT** hook `WebSocket.prototype.send`.
* **Outbound Observation Finding**:
  Because `WebSocket.prototype.send` is NOT instrumented in `pageBridge.ts`, the browser extension cannot capture outgoing `CLIENT → SERVER` frames at runtime.
* **Evidence Source Classification for `create_series`**:
  The reference to `create_series` in CP33.1.11 was derived from TradingView protocol specification patterns and inference from incoming `timescale_update` series identifiers (`LEVEL 6 = INFERENCE`), rather than direct runtime stream interception (`LEVEL 1 = DIRECT RUNTIME NETWORK CAPTURE`).
